# ai-for-job-search

Openclaw directive to help you land a job. The team consists of:

- Researcher: Looking for quality roles and monitoring changing trends in the job landscape

- Portfolio builder: Creating attention-grabbing projects to boost your profile

- Resume author: refining your resume and tailoring different versions based on the role the Researcher finds

- Application coodinator: Helping you apply for roles and scheduling your prep and interview calendar

- Strategy officer: Coordinating your team and keeping you updated on your job search pipeline.

Take your time to review, before using. Resources for installation and secure configuration included.
